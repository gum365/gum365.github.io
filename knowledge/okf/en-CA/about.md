## GUM365

GUM365 stands for **Microsoft 365 User Group**. The community is based in Montréal and organizes gatherings around the Microsoft 365 ecosystem, modern collaboration, information governance and artificial intelligence.

The goal is not to recreate a commercial conference. Community events exist first to let practitioners share experience, lessons, methods and questions.

## A member-driven community

Meetings exist because people propose sessions, participate in discussions, help organize, host the community and make events possible.

You can contribute by attending a gathering, proposing a session, helping from time to time or simply sharing your experience with other members.

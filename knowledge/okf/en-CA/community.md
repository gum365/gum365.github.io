## Our mission

GUM365 creates a place where professionals can **compare practices, share what actually works and learn from one another**. Product announcements are useful, but they never replace the experience of people who have to make technology work inside real organizations.

## Topics that bring us together

- Microsoft 365 and digital workplaces.
- SharePoint, document management, intranets and information architecture.
- Microsoft Teams and collaboration.
- Power Platform, automation and low-code development.
- Microsoft 365 Copilot, agents and artificial intelligence.
- Information governance, security, compliance and adoption.
- Knowledge architecture and turning content into usable information.

## An open community

The community naturally focuses on Microsoft 365, but it is not closed in on itself. When a technology, practice or method helps solve a collaboration, knowledge or work problem more effectively, it can contribute to the discussion.

We also want to make it easier for **new speakers** to get started. You do not need to be an MVP, author or professional speaker to share relevant experience.

## In-person exchange and continuity

In-person events remain central because a community is more than content delivery. Meetings are opportunities to learn, create connections, ask questions, compare ideas and keep talking after the sessions.

Between events, Meetup and other community channels help maintain continuity.

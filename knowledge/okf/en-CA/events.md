## M365 Community Days Montréal

**M365 Community Days Montréal** are the community's quarterly gathering. They focus on the Microsoft 365 ecosystem and the subjects that directly affect work inside organizations: SharePoint, Power Platform, Teams, Copilot, governance, knowledge, adoption and automation.

Each edition brings speakers and attendees together around technical sessions, demonstrations and practitioner experience.

For the 2026-2027 season, currently published dates include **October 7, 2026** and **January 27, 2027**. Official dates, locations and event status are maintained on Meetup and Sessionize.

## AI Community Days

**AI Community Days** are bilingual French/English gatherings dedicated to practical artificial intelligence, responsible AI and knowledge sharing. The format can combine in-depth workshops and conference days.

## SharePint

When scheduling allows, events continue over lunch or a **SharePint**. This is often where questions become more specific, experiences get compared and new collaborations begin.

## Call for speakers

The stage is open to anyone with useful experience to share: projects, migrations, instructive failures, automation, architecture, adoption, governance or experimentation.

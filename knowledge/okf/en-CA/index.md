## A community for learning, sharing and meeting people

GUM365 is the **Microsoft 365 User Group** in Montréal. The community brings together IT professionals, architects, consultants, managers, developers, trainers and enthusiasts who want to exchange practical experience around Microsoft 365 and the evolution of digital work.

Microsoft 365 is our natural home, but the community remains open to technologies, methods and practices that help people collaborate, automate, structure information and use artificial intelligence in useful and responsible ways.

## Our gatherings

### M365 Community Days Montréal

**M365 Community Days Montréal** are GUM365's quarterly gatherings. Each edition features a morning of technical sessions and experience sharing around Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Power Platform, Microsoft Teams, Copilot, information governance and knowledge architecture.

Content matters, but conversations matter too. When possible, meetings continue over a **SharePint** so discussions can carry on in a more informal setting.

### AI Community Days

GUM365 also supports **AI Community Days**, bilingual events dedicated to practical AI, responsible AI and knowledge sharing. Formats can combine hands-on workshops, conference sessions, demonstrations and practitioner experience.

## The stage is open

Community events are not reserved for Microsoft MVPs or experienced speakers. A concrete experience, an interesting project, a success or a lesson worth sharing can make an excellent session.

Visit the [Sessionize call for speakers](https://sessionize.com/gum365-communitydays-trimestriels/) to submit a topic.
